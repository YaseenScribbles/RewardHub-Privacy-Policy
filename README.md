# ESSA RewardHub Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 27 October 2026

ESSA RewardHub is the rewards app of **ESSA Garments Private Limited** ("ESSA", "we", "us"). Shops that
sell ESSA products earn points by scanning the QR codes on ESSA coupons and redeem those points for
gifts. This policy explains what we collect through the app, why, who we share it with, how long we
keep it, and how to ask us to delete it.

The app is for businesses (distributors and retailers) and the people who work for them. It is not
meant for children.

## What we collect

**About the shop**, when it registers or when our staff add it:
- shop name, whether it is a distributor or a retailer, and its address, city, state and pincode
- the shop's registered mobile number, and any other number or email it gives us
- its GST number and/or PAN, or its Aadhaar number if it has neither

**About each person who uses the app** for a shop:
- name, mobile number and, optionally, their role in the shop (for example, Manager)
- which shop they belong to and what the shop's admins allow them to do

**From using the app:**
- coupons scanned: which coupon, when, and by whom, and the points earned
- QR codes read that aren't ESSA coupons, cut to the first 50 characters, so we can tell why they
  weren't added
- photos of coupons whose QR code won't scan, taken with the phone's camera when the shop asks us to
  check one
- gifts redeemed, delivery addresses, and the parcels we send
- the gift the shop is saving for, and what is in its cart
- a sign-in record for each phone: the phone's model name and when it last signed in

**We don't collect** location, contacts, photos from the gallery, or advertising identifiers. The
app contains no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools.

## The camera

The app uses the camera only:
- to read the QR codes on coupons while you scan, and
- to take a photo of a coupon whose QR code won't scan, when you choose to send one to us.

QR codes are read on the phone. Only the code goes to us, and we never open it as a web link. Coupon
photos are taken with the camera, never chosen from the gallery, and are seen only by our staff.

## Why we use it

- to register shops and check them before they can earn points
- to let people sign in with a one-time code sent by SMS
- to credit points for coupons, check recovery requests, and prevent misuse of coupons, such as the
  same coupon being claimed twice
- to confirm redemptions with a code sent to the shop's registered number
- to send gifts, and to tell you about your shop, points and redemptions in the app
- to keep the business and tax records that the law requires

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

- **The shop's own team.** The shop's admins see its details and its team. What each employee can
  see is up to the admins. Aadhaar numbers are shown masked in the app (`XXXX XXXX 1234`).
- **ESSA staff**, who run the scheme in our back office, each with only the access their role needs.
- **Service providers** who act for us:
  - SMS Gateway Center, to send sign-in and confirmation codes to your mobile number
  - Hostinger, whose servers host the app's data
  - courier and transport companies, who receive the delivery name, phone number and address on a
    parcel
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

## How we protect it

The app talks to our servers only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Signing in needs a code sent
to your mobile number, and signing out, or being removed by your shop, ends that phone's access.
Coupon photos are stored privately and are never published. Inside ESSA, access is limited by role.

## How long we keep it

We keep a shop's records (its details, points, coupons, redemptions and parcels) for as long as the
shop is in the scheme, and afterwards for at least 6 years, as required by tax and accounting law.
Sign-in codes are handled by our SMS provider and expire after 5 minutes.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and correct your data.** The shop's details are in the app, under Profile → Shop details.
  Admins can change the contact details and address there. For anything else, email us.
- **Remove someone from a shop.** The shop's admins can remove a team member at any time in the app,
  under Profile → Team.
- **Delete your account.** See [Deleting your account](delete-account.md).
- **Complain.** Email us at the address above. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection
  Act, 2023, you may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change
affects you.
