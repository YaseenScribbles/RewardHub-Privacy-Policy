# Deleting your ESSA RewardHub account

**App:** ESSA RewardHub · **Developer:** ESSA Garments Private Limited

Anyone who uses ESSA RewardHub can ask us to delete their account.

## How to ask

**In the app:** go to Profile → Delete account.

**Without the app:** email customercare@essagarments.com and give:
- your name and the mobile number you sign in with
- your shop's name or code (for example, RTL0147)

We may call or text that number to confirm the request came from you. We handle requests within
30 days and tell you when it's done.

## What happens

**Deleted:**
- your sign-in. It stops working on every phone at once, and the number can no longer be used to
  sign in to that shop.
- your notifications, and the records of the phones you signed in on.

**Kept, as the shop's business records:**
- the shop's details, its points, the coupons it scanned, its redemptions and parcels
- your name next to the things you did for the shop (for example, "Scanned by …"), so its records
  stay complete

We keep these records for at least 6 years, as required by tax and accounting law, and then delete
them.

## If you are your shop's only admin

A shop always needs an admin. Before we delete your account, make someone else on your team an admin
(Profile → Team), or tell us who should be one. If nobody else uses the app for your shop, your
account is closed and the shop's records are kept as described above.
